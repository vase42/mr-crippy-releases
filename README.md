# MrCrippy

A cozy top-down pixel-art life sim: farm, fish, cook, build, and explore a little planet of themed
city districts with bars, cafés, trains and secrets.

## Download (Mac)

**[Download the latest version (1.2.0)](https://github.com/vase42/mr-crippy-releases/releases/tag/v1.2.0)**, then pick `MrCrippy-1.2.0.dmg`.

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag **MrCrippy** into **Applications**.
2. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't from the App Store: open **System Settings →
   Privacy & Security**, scroll down and click **Open Anyway** (or right-click the app → Open).
3. After that, MrCrippy updates itself. When a new version is out you'll see it on the title screen
   and under **Menu → Updates**.

Not on a Mac? Download `MrCrippy-1.2.0-web.zip`, unzip it and open `index.html` in a browser.
